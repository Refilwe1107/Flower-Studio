The Origins of FlowerStudio22

The FlowerStudio22!

This journey feels like destiny, as my name, “Palesa” meaning “flower” in Sesotho is a beautiful reflection of my lifelong passion for blooms and their powerful symbolism of growth, and joy. The Flower Studio is not just a business, it’s my heart, my pride, and my destiny brought to life. I’d be so grateful for your support whether by spreading the word, sharing the love, or finding the perfect bouquet to brighten your day or someone else's. Thank you for being part of this beautiful journey as fate and flowers come together in perfect harmony! With love and petals

Palesa
